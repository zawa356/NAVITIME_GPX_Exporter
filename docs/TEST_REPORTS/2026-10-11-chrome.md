## Outcome and limitations
**Chrome positive-path acceptance passed.** The user's Firefox-exported GPX and Chrome-exported GPX are exactly the same byte sequence. The separately retained private original Touring Supporter JSON was compared, yielding zero position/elevation/time mismatches over 2,779 points. This validates export fidelity, not the physical accuracy of the GPS or elevation recordings.

The Chrome screenshot demonstrates the **pre-export UI**, not the post-download state; the uploaded GPX is independent evidence of download completion. Filename suffix `(1)` is the browser's duplicate-download filename handling and is not part of the extension's generated filename.

**No Chrome store approval or public release is implied.** Other acceptance cases and Edge remain pending.
